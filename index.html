Communication Research

How do you think, reason, and communicate?

We're developing an application designed to help people strengthen their critical thinking and written communication skills through daily thought-provoking exercises.

Your responses will help us develop and evaluate an AI system that provides meaningful feedback on the quality of written arguments.

Five randomly assigned prompts
150–300 words suggested per response
All questions are optional
No names or email addresses collected
No account required

There are no right or wrong opinions. We're interested in how you communicate and support your ideas.

The survey is hosted on Google Forms.
